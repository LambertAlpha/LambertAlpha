# Boyi Lin (Lambert)

Data Science undergraduate at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen**

Visiting student at **UC Berkeley**, 2026–2027

I am interested in how language agents learn from interaction, generalize to new tasks,
and retain existing capabilities. My current focus is the relationship between feedback,
evaluation, and learning: when does a better score reflect a more capable agent?

## Research experience

During my summer 2026 research internship at **Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI)**,
I worked on training and evaluation tooling for agentic reinforcement learning. My work
included portable evaluation workflows, trajectory analysis, and investigating how scoring
and tool-use failures affect the feedback available to a learner. This led me to propose
process-based reward designs; their effect on training remains a question to test.

Previously, I led **Alpha-Hunter**, a team academic project exploring temporal representation
learning and auxiliary objectives for cross-sectional return prediction.
